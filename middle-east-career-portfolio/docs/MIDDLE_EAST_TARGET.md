### GCC CV conventions to research (do not assume)

- Whether to include nationality, visa status, date of birth, photo (common in some GCC CVs, but discriminatory in other markets — decide per channel; never on the public site without a decision).
- Preferred CV length and format for agencies vs direct applications.
- Whether attested qualifications are required for the visa category of each target role.
